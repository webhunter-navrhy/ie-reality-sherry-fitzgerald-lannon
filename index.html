Sherry FitzGerald Lannon are delighted to present this exceptional detached 5 Bedroom residence, ideally positioned within the exclusive development of Ballygarth Manor on the edge of Julianstown village. This impressive 'B' Building Energy Rated home, with Smart thermostat heating controls, extends to Approx. 244m²/2,625ft² and is presented in immaculate showhouse condition, having recently been further enhanced with the installation of solar panels with a 20kWh 'Sigenergy' battery and automatic mains backup.

From the moment you enter, the exceptional quality, attention to detail and elegant style of this beautiful family home are immediately evident. Four reception rooms are arranged off the impressive double height entrance hall, featuring luxurious wainscot wall panelling, which flows seamlessly into a generous open plan Kitchen/Dining/Living space and Sun Room, with direct access to the professionally landscaped rear garden and patio area, perfectly suited to both entertaining and relaxed outdoor living. Further highlights include a superb kitchen with granite and marble worktops, a large fully fitted Utility room and newly refurbished Bathroom, WC and Ensuite, combining comfort, style and practicality throughout.

Externally, the property benefits from a detached, block-built workshop (Approx. 8m²/85ft²), complete with an electricity supply and offering excellent potential for use as a home office or garden room. There are also three outdoor taps, including one with a hot water supply. To the front, the property is wired for an EV charging point and features a substantial cobble-block driveway, providing ample off-street parking.

This outstanding family home enjoys a superb location within easy walking distance of excellent amenities, including the newly renovated and extended Whitecross National School, bus stops serving Bus Éireann and Matthew's Coaches Dublin express service, Julianstown Golf and Pitch & Putt Club and the highly regarded Matt the Thresher Gastropub. The location also offers easy access to a host of nearby beaches, just a 2 or 3 minute drive away, while Drogheda is approximately 7km and access to the M1 motorway via Junction 7 is a mere 4km. Dublin Airport and the M50 orbital motorway are both accessible in approximately 20 minutes.

This prestigious and conveniently located residence is undoubtedly one of the finest and most substantial family homes to come to the market in Julianstown in recent times. Viewing is strictly by appointment only with sole agents.
